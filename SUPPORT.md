[Anything privacy policy](https://callcraftstudio.com/anything/privacy/).

Open **More actions → Help → Report a bug** in Anything. Describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Use sample filenames and leave out personal information.

You can preview and include optional diagnostics: app and Android versions, index counts, recent app events, and recent exit reasons on Android 11 and later. Root events include scanner outcomes and error counts, not command output. Anything does not request root to collect diagnostics or read system logs.

Open the addressed email draft, or copy the report and paste it into the [CallCraft Studio contact form](https://callcraftstudio.com/contact/). Review it before sending. If you're reporting outside the app, include the app version, Android version, Full or Play distribution, and storage mode.
